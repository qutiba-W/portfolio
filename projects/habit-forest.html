Habit Forest

Gamified habit tracker built in Next.js. Complete habits, grow your forest — with a study timer, mood and sleep tracking, and an XP system.

The Problem

Most habit trackers are lists. They record whether you did a thing, but they give you nothing back — no visual consequence for missing a streak, no reward for showing up. Habit Forest adds emotional stakes: your virtual forest grows when you complete habits and stops growing when you don't. The visual state of the forest becomes a proxy for your consistency.

The System

XP and leveling engine

Completing habits awards XP. XP accumulates into levels, providing immediate feedback on long-term consistency rather than just today's completion state. Streaks compound the reward.

Integrated trackers

Study timer (Pomodoro-style), mood logging, and sleep tracking sit alongside habit completion — keeping all productivity signals in one place without switching apps.

Full-stack with Firebase

Next.js 14 + TypeScript frontend. Firebase Authentication for accounts, Firestore for real-time data sync across devices. Social leaderboard for accountability between users.
