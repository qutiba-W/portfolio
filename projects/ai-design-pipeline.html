AI-Driven Design Pipeline

Framework integrating LLM Agents to parse technical requirements and streamline design-to-prototype workflows, reducing iteration time by 40%.

auto_awesome

psychology The Bottleneck

The translation of vague client requirements into actionable engineering specifications is often the most time-consuming part of the design process. Misunderstandings here lead to costly rework later. I sought to automate this parsing using Large Language Models to act as an initial filter and architect.

smart_toy The Methodology

I structured a multi-agent workflow where specialized AI agents handle different stages of the pipeline:

  • check_circle Requirement Agent: Parses natural language inputs to extract key constraints (material, load, size).
  • check_circle Ideation Agent: Generates multiple conceptual approaches based on the constraints.
  • check_circle Validation: The output is a structured specification sheet ready for human engineer review, cutting down the "blank page" phase significantly.
