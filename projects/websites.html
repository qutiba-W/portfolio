The three builds

Each of these is deployed and clickable. Together they are the evidence behind everything above.

NeuroForge

Live

Brain training PWA with six cognitive game modes and offline support — runs in the browser with no install. Vanilla JS, no framework. Sound cues are generated programmatically through the Web Audio API rather than shipped as audio files, and a service worker caches the whole app for offline play.

Vanilla JSPWAService workerWeb Audio API

→ neuroforge-brain.web.app

C2C Club

Live

Website for the Campus to Career Club at AUIS — events, an RSVP system, success stories, and a full Arabic/English toggle with RTL. Behind it sits a Firestore-backed CMS so non-technical admins can manage every piece of content without touching code.

HTML/JSFirebaseFirestorePWA

→ c2c-auib.web.app

Habit Forest

Live

Gamified habit tracker in Next.js 14 and TypeScript. Completing habits awards XP and grows a virtual forest; miss a streak and it stops growing. A study timer, mood and sleep logging, and a social leaderboard sit on Firebase Auth and real-time Firestore sync.
