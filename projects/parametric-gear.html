Parametric Gear System

Print-in-place mechanical assembly with 0.2mm tolerance using additive manufacturing. A study in clearance and functional assembly directly from the build plate.

settings_suggest

psychology_alt The Objective

Designing for additive manufacturing (DFAM) requires a deep understanding of printer capabilities and material behavior. The goal was to create a functioning gear train that could be printed as a single assembled unit ("print-in-place") without fusing moving parts together.

lightbulb Technical Details

I engineered the gear tooth profiles with specific clearances to accommodate the thermal expansion of PLA filament and the resolution of FDM printing.

  • check_circle 0.2mm Tolerance: Precise gap engineering allowed the gears to break free and rotate smoothly immediately after printing.
  • check_circle Interference Detection: Rigorous virtual testing in SolidWorks to ensure no mesh overlaps prior to slicing.
